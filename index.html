Essentials

🛂

Visa

Most European passport holders can apply for a free eVisitor (subclass 651) visa online. It allows stays of up to 3 months and is usually approved within minutes.

Apply for eVisitor 651
📱

Mobile & eSIM

Get a prepaid eSIM before you arrive (~AUD $30–50 for 30 days). Airalo, Holafly, and Saily all offer easy Australian eSIM plans with good 4G/5G coverage. Alternatively, buy a Telstra or Optus prepaid SIM at the airport.

ℹ️

Key Info

Currency: Australian Dollar (AUD), cards accepted almost everywhere. Time: AEDT (UTC+11) in Sydney in summer. Driving: LEFT side of the road. Emergency: 000. Power: Type I plug (three-pin). Sun: SPF 50+ is essential. Always carry water, especially in summer. If you have a boat licence (Sportbootführerschein), bring it.

Sydney: warm and humid, 22–30°C, occasional thunderstorms. Melbourne: warm and changeable, 15–30°C, four seasons in a day. Queensland: hot and tropical in the north, 26–34°C, wet season with possible cyclones. South Australia: hot and dry, 20–35°C. Western Australia: scorching in the north, pleasant in Perth, 20–38°C. Tasmania: mild and fresh, 14–24°C. Northern Territory: wet season, hot and humid, 25–35°C, spectacular storms.

🐨
Drop Bears
Australians will warn you about deadly "drop bears" — carnivorous koalas that fall from trees. They don't exist. Probably.
🪃
Oldest Civilisation
Aboriginal Australians have the world's oldest continuous civilisation — over 65,000 years of history, art, and culture.
🎄
Upside-Down Christmas
Christmas in Australia falls in the middle of summer. Think BBQs on the beach, prawns, and 35°C heat — not snow.
🗺️
Size vs Europe
Australia is roughly the same size as mainland Europe, but with only 26 million people. Most of the interior is vast, empty outback.
🏖️
10,000+ Beaches
With over 10,000 beaches, you could visit a new one every day for more than 27 years. Some are still completely untouched.
☕
Coffee Culture
Australians take coffee very seriously. Order a "flat white" — invented here (sorry, New Zealand). Starbucks failed spectacularly.
🦘
Aussie Slang
"No worries, mate" is basically the national motto. Australians shorten everything: afternoon = arvo, breakfast = brekky, McDonald's = Maccas.
🕷️
Deadly Reputation
Yes, Australia has dangerous wildlife, but fatalities are incredibly rare. You're far more likely to get sunburnt than bitten. Wear sunscreen.

Journey & Stopovers

The flight from Europe to Australia is long — typically 20–24 hours with one stop. Most routes pass through incredible cities worth a 1–2 day stopover, either on the way out or on the way home.

🏙️

Dubai

World-class shopping, the Burj Khalifa, desert safaris, and incredible food. Emirates flies direct from most European cities. A great 1–2 day stopover.

🌴

Singapore

A foodie paradise with stunning architecture, Gardens by the Bay, and hawker centres. Singapore Airlines is consistently rated the world's best. Perfect for 1–2 days.

🛕

Bangkok

Temples, street food, floating markets, and vibrant nightlife. Incredibly affordable and endlessly exciting. Great for 1–2 days.

🕌

Doha

The Museum of Islamic Art, the Souq Waqif, desert adventures, and superb dining. Qatar Airways offers easy connections. A solid 1–2 day stop.

Flight tip: These stopovers work equally well on the way out or on the way home. Book an open-jaw ticket (e.g. fly into Sydney, out of Auckland or Cairns) for maximum flexibility.

Sydney (10–14 Days)

Sydney is where we live and where most of you will spend the majority of your time. It's a world-class city with one of the most beautiful harbours on Earth, incredible beaches, outstanding food, and a relaxed outdoor lifestyle. Here are our top 20 must-do experiences.

01 Bondi to Coogee Coastal Walk

One of Sydney's most iconic walks — a 6km trail along dramatic sandstone cliffs, past stunning ocean pools and hidden beaches. Start at Bondi Icebergs, wind through Tamarama and Bronte, and finish with a drink at Coogee Pavilion.

02 Sydney Opera House Tour

No trip to Sydney is complete without seeing the Opera House up close. Book a guided tour or catch a performance. Book at sydneyoperahouse.com.

03 Harbour Bridge Walk & The Rocks

Walk across the Harbour Bridge via the pedestrian path for free. Then explore The Rocks — Sydney's oldest neighbourhood with cobblestone laneways, weekend markets, and colonial pubs. Grab a pale ale at Squires Landing on the water.

04 Manly Ferry

Take the iconic ferry from Circular Quay — the 30-minute ride is an experience in itself. At Manly, walk the Corso to the beach, rent a surfboard, or do the Spit to Manly walk (10km, stunning).

05 Taronga Zoo

Ferry across to Taronga Zoo for Australian wildlife against one of the world's best backdrops. See koalas, platypus, kangaroos, and the Sky Safari cable car ride is a highlight.

06 Blue Mountains Day Trip

A must-do, about 90 minutes west. The Three Sisters at Echo Point, the Scenic Railway (world's steepest), and endless hiking trails through ancient eucalyptus forest. Visit Wentworth Falls and stop in Leura for lunch.

07 Botanic Gardens → Mrs Macquarie's Chair → China Doll

Peaceful waterfront stroll through the Royal Botanic Gardens, continue to Mrs Macquarie's Chair for the iconic dual view, then walk along Woolloomooloo Wharf to China Doll for outstanding Asian-fusion lunch.

08 Seaplane from Rose Bay, Hermitage Foreshore & Watsons Bay

Fly a seaplane from Rose Bay over the harbour. Or walk the Hermitage Foreshore Track to Vaucluse. End at Watsons Bay — one of our favourite spots — great vibes, views, and food at the Watsons Bay Boutique Hotel.

09 Palm Beach & Barrenjoey Lighthouse

Sydney's northernmost beach. Climb to Barrenjoey Lighthouse for 360-degree views. This area is close to our wedding venue — you'll love it.

10 Barangaroo Sunset

Head to Barangaroo Reserve in the late afternoon and watch the sun set behind the Bridge. One of Sydney's best free experiences.

11 Sydney Fish Market

The third-largest fish market in the world. The freshest sushi, oysters, and grilled fish. Get there before 10am on weekends.

12 Harbour Kayaking

Paddle right past the Opera House and under the Harbour Bridge. Sunrise sessions are magical when the harbour is glassy.

13 Opera Bar Sunset

Grab a table at Opera Bar underneath the sails and watch the sun set over the Bridge. Friday nights have live music.

14 Darling Harbour Evening

Waterfront restaurants, bars, and the new harbourside precinct. Great area for a night out with dozens of venues.

15 Skydiving over Wollongong Beach

If not here, where? Tandem skydive from 15,000 feet over Wollongong's stunning coastline. Freefall for 60 seconds. An unforgettable experience.

16 Royal National Park Coast Track

Australia's oldest national park. Stunning clifftop walk past Wedding Cake Rock, through rainforest, to secluded beaches.

17 Hunter Valley Wine Tasting

Australia's oldest wine region, 2.5 hours north. Over 150 wineries producing outstanding Semillon and Shiraz. Book a day tour or stay overnight.

18 Rainy Day: Museums & Shows

If the weather turns: Art Gallery of NSW (free), Australian Museum, MCA, Powerhouse Museum, or a show at the Opera House.

19 Watsons Bay Sundowner

Take the ferry, stroll the cliff walk to South Head, then settle in at the Boutique Hotel as the sun drops. Fish and chips, a cold drink, golden light — perfection.

20 Secret Sydney: Hidden Gems

Swim at Mahon Pool in Maroubra, explore Newtown street art, catch live jazz at Venue 505, or take a dawn swim at North Curl Curl rock pool.

Grounds of Alexandria — Garden café in a converted warehouse. Kevin Bacon the pig lives in the courtyard.

Devon Café — Creative, beautifully plated brunch in Surry Hills.
Sydney Fish Market — The freshest seafood in the southern hemisphere. Buy and eat by the water.
China Doll — Outstanding Asian-fusion on Woolloomooloo Wharf with harbour views.

Coogee Pavilion — Multi-level venue on Coogee Beach. Great after the coastal walk.
Bisteca — Two-level Italian steakhouse in the CBD.

Around the Wedding Venue

Pasadena Sydney is on the beautiful Northern Beaches at Church Point — one of Sydney's hidden gems. Here's what to explore nearby.

Church Point & Scotland Island

Take the ferry to Scotland Island — a car-free island community with a wonderful local pub. It feels like stepping into another world.

Palm Beach & Barrenjoey Lighthouse

Just up the road. Iconic beach, Home & Away filming location, and the lighthouse walk with panoramic views.

Ku-ring-gai Chase National Park

Right on the doorstep. West Head Lookout, America Bay Track to a hidden waterfall, Aboriginal rock engravings.

Whale Beach

Quieter, more exclusive beach. Golden sand, turquoise water, fewer crowds. Whale Beach Deli for lunch.

Pittwater Kayaking

Rent a kayak at Church Point and paddle calm waters past secluded beaches, bushland shores, and houseboats.

New South Wales

The NSW South Coast is one of Australia's most underrated stretches of coastline.

If you're around our area, say hi to us in Oyster Bay or meet us at Cronulla Beach — our local beach!

Grand Pacific Drive

Scenic route south crossing the stunning Sea Cliff Bridge clinging to cliffs above the Pacific. Continue to Kiama, famous for its blowhole.

Jervis Bay & Hyams Beach

Hyams Beach holds the Guinness Record for the whitest sand in the world. Crystal-clear water, dolphins, outstanding snorkelling. Visit Booderee National Park nearby.

Kangaroo Valley & Berry

Picturesque green valley with the historic Hampden Bridge and canoeing. Berry has excellent cafés and the famous Berry Donut Van.

Port Stephens

Famous for dolphin watching (~160 bottlenose dolphins), massive sand dunes for sandboarding, and whale watching (May–November).

Cape Byron & Byron Bay

Australia's most easterly point. Walk to the lighthouse for whale spotting, surf at The Pass, and soak up the bohemian vibe.

Victoria

Melbourne & Laneways

Australia's cultural capital — world-famous street art in Hosier Lane, incredible coffee, buzzing laneways with hidden bars. Don't miss Queen Victoria Market.

St Kilda Penguins

Every evening at dusk, little penguins waddle ashore at St Kilda Pier. Free to watch, utterly charming. Combine with a sunset stroll.

Yarra Valley

Melbourne's wine region, just an hour east. Outstanding Pinot Noir and Chardonnay, plus Healesville Sanctuary for native wildlife.

Phillip Island Penguins

The famous Penguin Parade — hundreds of little penguins emerge from the sea at sunset. Magical, especially the premium viewing platforms. ~90 minutes from Melbourne.

One of the world's most scenic coastal drives. The Twelve Apostles are the showstopper, but the entire route is breathtaking — rainforest, surf beaches, dramatic cliffs.

Important: The Great Ocean Road is a LONG day trip from Melbourne — allow 12–14 hours round trip. Depart very early (6–7am). Consider staying overnight in Lorne or Apollo Bay.

Queensland

Great Barrier Reef

The world's largest coral reef. Snorkel or dive from Cairns or Port Douglas. Choose an outer reef trip for the best visibility. A scenic helicopter flight is unforgettable.

Sea-Doo Ride

Explore the tropical coastline on a jet ski. No licence needed in Queensland — book through a local operator in Cairns or Port Douglas.

Daintree Rainforest

The world's oldest tropical rainforest (180+ million years old). Cape Tribulation where rainforest meets reef. Overnight stays are magical.

Atherton Tablelands & Port Douglas

Stunning waterfalls (Millaa Millaa, Zillie), crater lakes, platypus spotting. Port Douglas has Four Mile Beach and excellent Sunday Markets.

Whitehaven Beach & Hill Inlet

This is my absolute favourite place in Australia. Whitehaven is 7km of the purest white silica sand you've ever seen, and Hill Inlet — where the tide swirls sand and turquoise water into ever-changing patterns — is one of the most beautiful things on Earth. Take a seaplane for the aerial view, or stay on a sailing yacht overnight. Trust me on this one. — Fabian

Skydiving over Airlie Beach

Skydive over the Whitsundays with views of the islands, reef, and Whitehaven Beach below. Pretty hard to beat.

Burleigh Heads & Gold Coast

Burleigh is the Gold Coast's coolest neighbourhood — great surf, the headland walk, and a buzzing café scene. The hinterland has Springbrook and Lamington National Parks.

South Australia

Adelaide & Hahndorf

Adelaide is charming, walkable, with outstanding food. Hahndorf — Australia's oldest German settlement (1839) — is a surreal taste of home for our German guests: German bakeries, beer halls, and a main street that could be in Bavaria. Try the Bienenstich at the German Cake Shop.

Barossa Valley

One of the world's great wine regions. Famous for bold Shiraz — some vines are over 150 years old. Visit Penfolds, Henschke, and Seppeltsfield.

McLaren Vale & d'Arenberg Cube

Stunning wine region with 80+ cellar doors. The d'Arenberg Cube is a striking Rubik's Cube-shaped building housing wine experiences, a restaurant with panoramic views, and interactive art.

Kangaroo Island

Australia's Galápagos. Sea lions at Seal Bay, wild koalas, Remarkable Rocks. Allow at least 2 days. Outstanding local produce — honey, gin, oysters.

Western Australia

Perth, Kings Park & Fremantle

Perth is relaxed and sunny with outstanding beaches. Kings Park has sweeping views. Fremantle has historic markets, craft breweries, and great seafood.

Rottnest Island & Quokkas

Car-free island paradise. Bike between pristine bays, snorkel, and meet the famous quokkas — the world's happiest animal. The quokka selfie is an Australian rite of passage.

Pinnacles & Lancelin Sand Dunes

Otherworldly limestone pillars in the desert. Lancelin has massive dunes for sandboarding. Nothing beats tearing across sand on a BRP quad bike — ATV tours are an absolute blast.

Margaret River

World-class wine region with outstanding Cabernet Sauvignon, stunning coastline, ancient caves, and excellent surfing.

For those with more time, the coast north of Perth is among the most spectacular and least visited on Earth.

Ningaloo Reef

Australia's other great reef — snorkel right from the shore. Swim with whale sharks (March–July), manta rays, and humpback whales.

Coral Bay & Turquoise Bay

Coral Bay has reef just metres from the beach. Turquoise Bay's drift snorkel is extraordinary.

Shark Bay & Shell Beach

UNESCO World Heritage area. Shell Beach — billions of tiny white shells stretching 100km. Wild dolphin interactions at Monkey Mia.

Hutt Lagoon Pink Lake

Bubblegum-pink lake, most vivid in summer/February — when you'll be here. Surreal and utterly photogenic.

Northern Territory

Uluru Sunset & Field of Light

Uluru at sunset glows deep red, orange, and purple. The Field of Light installation transforms the desert into 50,000 glowing stems at night.

Base Walk & Kata Tjuta

Walk the 10km base trail to understand Uluru's true scale. Kata Tjuta's Valley of the Winds walk offers dramatic views through towering red domes.

Kings Canyon

The rim walk (6km) along 100m-high sandstone walls. The Garden of Eden — a permanent waterhole surrounded by lush vegetation — is surreal.

Darwin & Kakadu National Park

Tropical frontier city with outstanding Asian food (Mindil Beach Sunset Market). Kakadu has ancient rock art, dramatic waterfalls, and saltwater crocodiles.

Note: February is wet season in the Top End. Expect heat, humidity, and spectacular storms. Some areas may be closed due to flooding, but the waterfalls are at their most powerful.

New Zealand

For those keen to "cross the ditch" — New Zealand is just a 3-hour flight from Sydney and offers some of the most dramatic scenery on the planet.

Auckland & Waiheke Island

Auckland sits between two harbours. Ferry to Waiheke — a wine island with vineyards, olive groves, and beautiful beaches.

Hobbiton & Rotorua

The Hobbiton movie set is a must for LOTR fans. Rotorua has Māori culture, bubbling mud pools, geysers, and hāngī feasts.

Tongariro Alpine Crossing

One of the world's great day walks — 19.4km across volcanic terrain past emerald lakes and active craters. Allow 6–8 hours.

Queenstown

Adventure capital of the world on Lake Wakatipu. Bungee jumping, skydiving, jet boating, and some of the best scenery you'll ever see.

Milford Sound

A fiord of impossible beauty — towering cliffs, cascading waterfalls, seals on the rocks. A boat cruise is essential.

Wanaka & Roy's Peak

Quieter alternative to Queenstown. Roy's Peak hike (1,578m) has 360-degree views. Allow 5–6 hours return.

Mount Cook & Hooker Valley

New Zealand's highest peak. The Hooker Valley Track is a 10km walk to a glacial lake with swing bridges and icebergs.

Kaikoura — Whale Watching

Year-round sperm whale watching, swimming with dolphins, and incredibly fresh crayfish.

Green-lipped mussels, lamb (some of the world's best), hāngī (Māori feast), pavlova (they say it's theirs), L&P lemonade, and outstanding Pinot Noir from Central Otago.

Exotic Australian Food

One standout restaurant in each city showcasing something you won't find anywhere else.

Sydney — Nour

Modern Middle Eastern fine dining using native Australian ingredients through a Lebanese lens.

Melbourne — Charcoal Lane

Social enterprise serving native cuisine — kangaroo, emu, wattleseed, bush tomato.

Cairns — Ochre

Pioneer of Australian native cuisine. Try crocodile, barramundi, and bush herbs.

Adelaide — Africola

Bold, smoky African-inspired food with South Australian produce.

Perth — Wildflower

Rooftop restaurant showcasing West Australian ingredients through the six Noongar seasons.

Packing & Tips

☀️

Sun Protection

SPF 50+ sunscreen (reef-safe), wide-brim hat, quality sunglasses, rash vest. Australian UV is extreme. Reapply every 2 hours.

💧

Hydration

Always carry water. Bring a reusable bottle — tap water is safe throughout Australia. In the outback, carry more than you think.

🔋

Powerbank

20,000mAh portable charger. Long days exploring drain your phone fast. Essential for day trips and remote areas.

🔌

Power Adapter

Australia uses Type I plugs (three-pin). Bring a universal adapter or buy one at the airport.

📷

Waterproof Phone Case

Essential for beaches, snorkelling, kayaking, jet skiing. Under $20 and saves you from expensive accidents.

🚤

Boat Licence

If you have a Sportbootführerschein, bring it — Fabian might take you out on the Sea-Doos.

🏥

Travel Insurance

Get comprehensive cover for medical expenses and adventure activities like skydiving, jet skiing, and hiking.

💳

Notify Your Bank

Tell your bank and credit card provider about travel to Australia. Consider a card with no foreign transaction fees.

🚗

Driving Licence

Bring your licence plus an International Driving Permit (IDP). From ADAC in Germany. Drive on the LEFT!

📱

Useful Apps

TripView (Sydney transport), Google Maps (download offline maps), Uber/DiDi, Opal Travel.

Flights: Qantas, Virgin Australia, and Jetstar connect all major cities. Book early. Sydney to Melbourne ~1.5 hours, Sydney to Cairns ~3 hours.

Driving: Drive on the LEFT. Distances are vast — Sydney to Melbourne is ~9 hours by road. International Driving Permit recommended.

Sydney transit: Get an Opal card (or use contactless credit card) for trains, buses, ferries, and light rail. Uber and DiDi widely available.
